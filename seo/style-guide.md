# Style guide

How the site writes. Every page should sound like one knowledgeable, honest local business talking to a busy Dubai resident.

Related: [content-structure.md](content-structure.md), [qa-checklist.md](qa-checklist.md).

## Voice

- **Direct and plain.** Short sentences, everyday words. Say what happens, who does it and what the reader needs to do.
- **Practical and specific to Dubai.** Service lifts, NOCs, gate passes, Friday traffic, community rules and the free municipal pickup. Specifics prove we know the job.
- **Honest about limits.** Say what we don't do: hazardous waste, data destruction, felling trees, guaranteed same-day slots. That builds trust.
- **Warm, not cute.** A light line is fine in headlines ("Palm fronds don't fit in the bin. They fit on our truck."). Body copy stays practical.
- **"We" for the business, "you" for the reader.**

## Language

- **British English:** colour, organise, licence (noun), metre, kerb, storey.
- Write dates as "4 November 2026" and times as "7am to 10pm".
- **Currency:** "AED 500". Only quote figures the owner has confirmed.
- **Numbers:** spell out one to nine in prose, and use digits for 10 and above, measurements and floors ("14th floor").
- Use "Dubai Municipality" in full on first mention.
- **Area names** match `src/data/areas.ts` exactly (for example, "JVC", "Al Barsha").

## Formatting

- **Paragraphs:** 1–3 sentences. One idea each.
- **Lists:** only for real lists, such as steps, items or options. Keep items parallel and short.
- **Callouts:** for one important warning or tip per section at most.
- **Bold:** sparingly, for the single phrase a skimmer must not miss.
- **No ALL CAPS, emojis or exclamation marks** in body copy.
- **Headings:** sentence case ("Where it doesn't reach").

## Accuracy and originality

- **Fact-check anything outside the business's control:** municipal rules, phone numbers, facilities, recycling options, permits. Cite the source in the guide's `sources`.
- **Don't state as fact what we don't know.** Use "check with your building management" rather than inventing a rule.
- **Never invent:**
  - customer counts or job totals
  - reviews or ratings
  - licences or certifications
  - awards
  - prices or response times
  - statistics or search data

  These come only from the owner, verified.
- **Write from the job, not from other websites.** Don't paraphrase competitors. Every page must say something only this business would say.
- **No content generated to hit a length.** If a section adds nothing, cut it.

## Human-first rules

- **Answer first:** the opening sentence of a guide answers the question.
- **Write for one reader with one problem.** If a paragraph would fit any city or any business, rewrite it with Dubai specifics or delete it.
- **Recommend the free option when it genuinely fits** (as the municipal pickup guide does). Honest advice is the site's trust advantage.
- **Keep calls to action helpful:** one natural mention of the service where it solves the reader's problem. The page template already handles the rest.
- **Show experience from real jobs.** One real example per guide (area, problem, how the crew solved it) beats any amount of general advice. It must come from the owner or crew. Never invent a job, a customer or a quote.
- **Write sensitive topics gently.** Bereavement (#48) and hoarding (#59) guides are practical and kind. No humour, no sales pressure, no judgement.
- **Speed doesn't change the rules.** Two guides a day is the plan. A guide that isn't ready waits.

## Keywords

- Use the page's primary keyword in the title, the H1 or opening, and the description, **once each, naturally.**
- After that, write for the reader. Use natural variants (removal, collection, clearing, getting rid of) and related topics.
- If a sentence exists mainly to fit a keyword in, delete it.

## Claims that need the owner's confirmation

The site currently states these as service promises. Confirm they stay true before repeating them in new content:

- fixed price agreed from a photo before work starts
- payment after the job
- free quotes, no call-out fee
- crews dismantle, carry from any floor and sweep up
- reuse and recycling where possible, with nothing dumped
- open Saturday to Thursday 7am–10pm, Friday 9am–10pm
- same-day **when available**, never guaranteed
- crews take down freestanding pallet racking and shelving, then load and remove it (owner confirmed 18 November 2026)

## Words to avoid

- "best", "No. 1", "cheapest" or "leading", without proof
- "eco-friendly" or "100% recycled", which can't be verified
- "guaranteed" for timing
- "licensed", "insured" or "certified" about the business, until the owner confirms it with documents
- "near me" in titles or headings
- filler such as "In today's fast-paced world" or "Look no further"
- keyword strings such as "junk removal Dubai junk removal services Dubai"
